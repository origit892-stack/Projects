# THE OUTSKIRTS — Game Vision

## Identity

**Game:** THE OUTSKIRTS

THE OUTSKIRTS is an atmospheric survival game. The target is a polished,
cohesive experience rather than a basic Roblox blockout or a collection of
unconnected systems.

## Target Experience

The player should feel vulnerable, curious, and motivated to make an abandoned
place functional again. Environments, gameplay systems, lighting, sound, UI,
and progression should ultimately support the same survival atmosphere.

The intended quality bar is:

- atmospheric
- detailed
- intentionally composed
- abandoned but functional
- survival-oriented
- visually cohesive
- significantly more polished than the current bunker

## Starting Experience

The player wakes near an old bed inside a creepy, abandoned underground bunker
bedroom.

The bedroom is the first readable space in a larger bunker. It must establish:

- where the player has awakened
- that the bunker is old, neglected, and still partly functional
- a clear visual route toward the rest of the bunker
- the survival tone of the wider game

The bedroom must connect into a larger bunker. It is not an isolated box and
must not be treated as a standalone final environment.

## Bunker Vision

The bunker should be a sequence of purposeful, connected spaces rather than one
rectangular room. The layout should eventually support:

- bedroom/start area
- central bunker/common area
- weapon/workbench area
- upgrade area
- storage
- power/electrical infrastructure
- NPC/shop area
- access toward farming and the outside
- bunker entrance/defense point

These are spatial and long-term functional requirements. Missing gameplay
behavior must not be invented during environment planning.

## Broader Gameplay Vision

Long-term concepts include:

- realistic-feeling rifle and combat gameplay
- pickaxe/tool gameplay
- torch/flashlight gameplay
- farming with multiple crops, including wheat and carrots
- improved zombies
- wave survival
- NPC shop
- progression
- polished UI
- multiple maps and biomes

These items describe direction only. They are not permission to implement a
system unless a specific approved task defines it.

## Quality and Scope Rules

- Do not treat the rejected bedroom blockout as approved composition or art.
- Its dimensions may be consulted only as rough reference.
- Do not let legacy geometry or presentation define the rebuilt bunker.
- Do not invent missing gameplay specifications.
- Separate layout approval, art direction, gameplay implementation, and polish
  into explicitly approved phases.
