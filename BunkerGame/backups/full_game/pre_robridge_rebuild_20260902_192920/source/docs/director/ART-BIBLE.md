# THE OUTSKIRTS — Art Bible

## Visual Goal

THE OUTSKIRTS should present a polished, atmospheric survival world with a
deliberate visual language. The final bunker must not look like plain cubes,
default Roblox presentation, or an arbitrary collection of props.

## Bunker Mood

The starting bunker is abandoned but functional. It should feel creepy,
industrial, aged, and believable without losing navigational clarity.

The starting bedroom should eventually communicate this through elements such
as:

- an aged bed
- locker or storage
- shelving
- believable bunker architecture
- pipes and cables
- industrial details
- cobwebs
- grime and wear
- a flickering fluorescent fixture
- emergency or utility lighting
- a later usable torch/flashlight
- a strong visual route toward the bunker interior

These are desired art-pass elements, not authorization to add them during a
layout or composition task.

## Environment Construction Direction

Future art passes should pursue:

- believable structural thickness
- layered materials
- trim and transitions between surfaces
- beams and supports
- pipes
- cables and conduits
- vents
- industrial fixtures
- purposeful clutter
- wear, grime, and age
- environmental storytelling
- controlled lighting
- strong silhouettes
- deliberate color palette
- high-quality composition

Architecture should read as constructed space, not thin planes or hollow cubes.
Detail should reinforce purpose, history, scale, and navigation.

## Composition Principles

- Give every major space a clear purpose and focal hierarchy.
- Use corridors, thresholds, lighting, and silhouettes to guide the player.
- Balance enclosed tension with readable circulation.
- Keep important routes legible even when the environment becomes detailed.
- Compose props as part of the room's function and history, not as scattered
  decoration.
- Use repetition and a controlled material/color family to make the bunker
  cohesive.

## Existing Art Status

The current bunker is functional reference, not final art. Its pavement shell,
asset geometry, materials, colors, and placement must not dictate the rebuild.

The previous `BedroomBlockout` is visually rejected. Its design and composition
are not approved.

## Phase Discipline

### Design/Composition Phase

Allowed:

- spatial planning
- room relationships
- circulation
- world placement
- scale and silhouette studies
- neutral blockout categories

Not allowed:

- final textures
- decals
- cobwebs
- detailed clutter
- final materials
- final lighting
- VFX
- art polish

### Art Pass

An art pass begins only after the director approves the layout and composition.
Art direction does not authorize gameplay implementation.
