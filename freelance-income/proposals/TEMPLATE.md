# Master proposal template — EN

> Rules: ≤150 words where the job allows; mirror their exact words in line 1; NO "passionate/dedicated/team player"; answer every screening question point-by-point (short answers); end with ONE specific technical question (starts conversations, filters tire-kickers); never promise to match a budget you can't hit.

---

**Line 1 (their problem, their words, one sentence):**
"You need {EXACT THING FROM THEIR POST} — I build and operate exactly this class of system in production, so you're hiring an operator, not a tinkerer."

**Line 2 (proof, 2 bullets max, real systems, no invented clients):**
Proof you can check today:
- {SYSTEM 1 — one line: what it does + that it runs unattended}
- {SYSTEM 2 — one line}
Public links: github.com/origit892-stack/Projects · openshelf.1st4offers.com

**Line 3 (approach, 3 steps):**
1. I map your workflow and send back the plan + fixed price + done-definition (24-48h).
2. I build with error handling, logging and idempotency — replayed webhooks never double-act.
3. Handover: source, docs, Loom walkthrough. {N} days of support included.

**Line 4 (price + time, only if the job states budget):**
"{THEIR BUDGET} is workable for {SCOPED PART} — I'll send the exact fixed price after the mapping step. Timeline: {X} days."
(If no budget stated: "Fixed price, quoted after the 24-48h mapping step. Timeline: {X} days.")

**Line 5 (risk line, what others skip):**
Every system ships after it has run in my own 24/7 production first — monitoring, failure alerts, checkpointed recovery. If it breaks after handover: a fix, not a ticket queue.

**Line 6 (close — one specific question):**
"{SPECIFIC QUESTION ABOUT THEIR STACK, e.g.: 'Is your CRM on GoHighLevel or HubSpot? That changes which integration path I build first.'}"
