## FAQ
1. **Q: Do I need to know anything technical?** A: No. I need access to the tools you want connected (email, CRM, sheets, WhatsApp) and a description of the task. I handle the rest and hand over with docs.
2. **Q: What if the agent makes a mistake?** A: Guardrails on (stays on topic, refuses to guess, flags low confidence), every action logged, and human handoff for anything sensitive. You approve the risky steps.
3. **Q: Can you work with my existing n8n/Zapier/Make setup?** A: Yes — I take over, fix and extend existing automations too (audit + fix is available as an add-on).
4. **Q: Do you offer ongoing support?** A: Yes, care plans from $150/mo: monitoring, fixes, small additions. Cancel anytime.
5. **Q: How do I know it works before I pay?** A: Fixed price, you pay on delivery after reviewing the Loom walkthrough and the test results. Support days are included for exactly this.
