# Free channels — replaces Upwork/Fiverr (user decision 2026-11-08)
User: "אני לא רוצה Fiverr ו-Upwork. תמצא תחליף חינמי." Verified research behind this doc:

## Marketplaces checked (honest verdict)
- **Braintrust** (usebraintrust.com) — **the only truly free marketplace for talent**: 0% talent fee (client pays 15% on top — client's cost, not ours). Rates $18-100/hr, AI skills interview at onboarding, invoicing via Stripe/Wise. CAVEAT: direct payouts cover ~35 countries (Israel — verify at signup; if not listed, only client-direct-pay roles are visible to you). Enterprise-leaning, but AI/automation roles exist.
- **Contra** — NOT free: "0% commission" marketing, but a flat $15 fee per client payment ($29 over $500) plus a reported $30/mo freelancer paywall (2026 reviews). OFF the list.
- **Malt** — NOT free for talent: 10% fee on first mission with a new client (5% after 6 months, 2% own clients). OFF the list.

**Rule:** any channel taking a cut of OUR revenue is off the list. Client-side fees (the client pays the platform) are fine — they never touch our money.
