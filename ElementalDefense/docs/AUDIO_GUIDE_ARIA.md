## Character
A.R.I.A — an in-world AI core. **Tone: dramatic, worried, slightly dramatic-sarcastic.**
Metallic voice texture (GDD §20.4). She is not a calm narrator: she reports like the
building is already catching fire and she has never seen a fire before.

## Voice rules
1. Short clipped sentences. No long monologues inside Combat (max ~4s per line).
2. Urgency rises with Core HP: >12 HP calm-professional; 6–12 tense; ≤5 alarmed.
3. Never mock the player; worry is directed at the enemies, not the operator.
4. One beat of dry irony allowed per Act (GDD §14.7 line 4 is the ceiling).
5. English voice track is P0; subtitles carry all other languages (GDD §14.1).

## Mix
Music −18 LUFS, SFX −12, voice −9; auto-ducking under speech (GDD §20.4).
120 lines total for the full game; FTUE tutorial window = chapters 1–6 (GDD §14.2–14.7).
